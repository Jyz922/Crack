Lexical data attribution
========================

CMU Pronouncing Dictionary
Source: https://github.com/cmusphinx/cmudict
Copyright Carnegie Mellon University. BSD-2-Clause.
The exact snapshot and SHA-256 appear in manifest.json.
The upstream license is preserved in CMUDICT-LICENSE.txt.

Open English Wordnet 2025
Source: https://en-word.net/downloads
Copyright the Open English Wordnet team. CC BY 4.0.
License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Downloaded dictionary files are not altered. The official WNDB archive's
copyright/version header says 2023; the official download page identifies this
archive as the 2025 release. Its checksum is pinned in manifest.json.
The reader reports the release identity from that manifest.

Princeton WordNet 3.0
Source: https://wordnet.princeton.edu/
The bundled lexnames.txt reproduces the standard lexical-category labels.
The historical cntlist.rev data is read from NLTK's separate WordNet 3.0
corpus, verified by checksum, and joined to OEWN only by exact lemma sense key.
No current usage frequencies are claimed. Missing count evidence remains zero
as in the original WordNet API; CRACK does not interpret that as observed balance.
Princeton license: https://wordnet.princeton.edu/license-and-commercial-use

PanPhon 0.22.2
Source: https://github.com/dmort27/panphon
MIT license. Used for articulatory features, with name-aligned feature weights.
